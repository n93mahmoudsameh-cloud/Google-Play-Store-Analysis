# 📊 Google Play Store Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Google Play Store Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Tableau Public**.

The project focuses on analyzing application data from the Google Play Store to identify trends and patterns related to app categories, ratings, reviews, installs, pricing, and app types.

The dashboard provides an interactive way to explore the data and extract meaningful insights from the Google Play Store dataset.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze the distribution of applications across different categories.
* Explore app ratings and review patterns.
* Analyze the number of installs across applications.
* Compare Free and Paid applications.
* Explore app prices and their relationship with performance.
* Identify popular and high-performing app categories.
* Build an interactive dashboard for data exploration and storytelling.

## 🔍 Analysis & Insights

The dashboard provides insights into several aspects of the Google Play Store, including:

### 📱 App Categories

Analysis of the number of applications across different categories and identification of the most represented categories.

### ⭐ Ratings

Exploration of app ratings and their distribution across different applications and categories.

### 💬 Reviews

Analysis of user reviews and their relationship with app popularity and performance.

### 📥 Installs

Comparison of installation levels across applications and categories to identify popular apps and segments.

### 💰 Free vs Paid Apps

Comparison between free and paid applications to understand differences in popularity and performance.

### 💵 Pricing

Analysis of paid application prices and their relationship with installs and ratings.
